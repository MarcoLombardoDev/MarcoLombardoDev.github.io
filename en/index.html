Curiosity is the engine, passion the fuel

I'm Marco Lombardo. My work lives at the intersection of technology, innovation and business: understanding how an organisation really works, spotting where friction hides, and designing the digital answer to it — from the first sketch to something people use every day.

I've always been the person who opens the box to see how it works. That curiosity turned into a career built on digital products, process automation and emerging technologies, and into a habit I never lost: prototyping ideas myself instead of only talking about them.

Outside of work the same instinct keeps going — experimenting with AI, writing code for the pleasure of it, creating content, and sharing the useful parts with anyone who might need them.

Four rules I keep coming back to

R-01

Start from the problem

Technology is the answer, never the question. If the problem isn't clear, no stack will save the project.

R-02

Ship, then refine

A working prototype beats a perfect presentation. Reality gives better feedback than any meeting.

R-03

Design is not decoration

How something looks and how it works are the same conversation. Clarity is a feature.

R-04

Share what you learn

Knowledge kept private stops growing. Everything I've built started from something someone else shared first.
