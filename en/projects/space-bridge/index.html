Game

Space Bridge

Manage your ship, lead your crew and win turn-based battles.

Privacy policy

Space Bridge is a ship-management game with turn-based battles for Android, set in the same universe as Space Warfront and the other games in the series. You are the commander of a Coalition ship, seen in cross-section with its rooms and the crew walking around inside: you build and upgrade rooms, put people in the right places, collect credits and components and set out on missions.

Battles are turn-based: you don't pilot the ship, you choose the moves. Each turn you spend the reactor's energy to play cards (Laser, Missiles, Boost shields, Evade, Boarding, Repair and the crew's special moves) on the enemy system of your choice, reading the enemy's intentions.

The story is Operation Bridge, the chain of beacons that carries reinforcements from Aster station to the fortress planet Drakmor: 12 missions with comic-style dialogue before and after, patrols to replay and events where every choice has a price or a reward. Then there is Battle, a series of ever harder fights, for the record. The game is single player and works without a connection.

  • Platform
    Android app, landscape only
  • Genre
    Ship management and turn-based battles
  • Modes
    12-mission Story, Battle, single player
  • Languages
    English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Russian
  • Technology
    TypeScript, Canvas 2D and Capacitor
What you can do
  • Build and upgrade 11 room types and grow the hull up to level six
  • Put six characters in the right rooms: they work better and bring their special move into battle
  • Fight turn by turn, playing cards with the reactor's energy and hitting enemy systems
  • Read the enemy's intentions and disable the system that is about to fire
  • Collect credits and components: the Refinery and the Workshop keep producing while the game is closed
  • Play 12 missions with comic-style dialogue and events with choices, then chase your record in Battle
  • Learn with the tutorial in a real battle, in sessions of five minutes or half an hour
  • Play without a connection, in six languages
