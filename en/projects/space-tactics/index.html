Space Tactics

Real-time space strategy, one base at a time.

Privacy policy

Space Tactics is a real-time space strategy game for Android, with the same painted look as Space Warfront. Each sector is a map of bases linked by hyperspace routes: every base keeps producing one type of ship on its own, and you capture neutral and enemy bases by sending more ships than they have defenders.

There are three kinds of ships: frigates, which only travel along the routes, fighters, which fly straight but only capture hangars, and torpedoes, which hit an area but cannot capture. Bases can be upgraded on three fronts (Defense, Anti-air and Capacity, each on three levels) and the commander has energy-based powers: Hyperdrive, Orbital Bombardment, Reinforcements and Sabotage.

The Campaign has 30 missions across 5 sectors and, between missions, the stars you earn are spent on Research. There are also Skirmish, an endless Survival mode with a record to beat, and same-Wi-Fi multiplayer for 2 to 4 players, free-for-all or in an Alliance against the AI.

  • Platform
    Android app, landscape only
  • Genre
    Real-time base-conquest strategy
  • Modes
    30-mission Campaign, Skirmish, Survival, Wi-Fi multiplayer
  • Languages
    Italian, English, French, German, Spanish, Russian
  • Technology
    TypeScript, Canvas 2D and Capacitor
What you can do
  • Capture bases by sending more ships than they have defenders, choosing how many to send (25%, 50% or 100%)
  • Play 30 missions across 5 sectors, with different goals: Conquer, Capture, Survive and Defend
  • Upgrade bases with Defense, Anti-air and Capacity and use the commander's four powers
  • Spend the stars you earn on Research to strengthen your fleets
  • Beat your record in the endless Survival mode, or play a free Skirmish
  • Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi, 2 to 4 players, free-for-all or in an Alliance
