Space Warfront Privacy Policy

How data is handled in the Space Warfront Android app. Last updated: November 3, 2026.

In short

Space Warfront has no accounts or servers and I do not collect personal data: your progress stays on your device and multiplayer works only over the local network. The game does show ads through Google AdMob, though: to show and measure them Google may process your advertising ID, IP address and some device and app data. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form appears first, and you can change your choice from the «Ad options» button (see the «Advertising» section). I do not receive this data.

Who I am and what this covers

The Space Warfront app (package ID app.spacewarfront) is developed and published by Marco Lombardo (MLDev), who for what is described on this page is also the data controller under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

For any question you can write to me at marco.lombardo@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Space Warfront Android app only. For the website this page is on, see the site's privacy page.

You can find it on the game's listing on Google Play and inside the app, from the «Privacy» link on the main screen.

What data I collect

I do not collect personal data: the app does not send personal data or usage data to me, and I have no servers, accounts, or analytics or crash-reporting tools. The only exception is advertising, explained in the «Advertising» section: the ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may process some data from your device; I do not receive it. In particular:

  • the app has no accounts and requires no sign-up;
  • it does not access precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or calendar;
  • it has no in-app purchases or chat;
  • the only third-party SDK is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob); the other libraries are only the ones needed to run the game on the device;
  • the only link that leads out of the app is the one to this policy, which opens in the browser.

Fonts, images, sounds and music are bundled with the app or generated on the device: the game itself downloads nothing from the internet. The internet connection is used only for ads.

Data stored on your device

To remember how you are playing, the app saves in the device's local storage:

  • the levels you have unlocked and the stars you have earned;
  • audio settings (music on or off, effects and music volume);
  • the language you chose, if you changed it;
  • the last settings chosen for the Battle and Arena modes and for hosting a network match, which ship cards you have already seen and whether you have completed the training.

The app also keeps a backup copy in the app's preferences, still on the device, so you don't lose your progress. This data is never sent to me or to anyone else, cannot be used to identify you, and stays on the device until you delete it: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Space Warfront → Storage → Clear data.

The app allows Android's automatic backup. If you have device backup turned on, Android may copy this data to your Google account, according to your settings and Google's policy; I have no access to those backups. If you don't want that, turn off backup in your device settings.

Permissions the app requests

The app requests only «normal» permissions, granted at install time without prompting you:

  • INTERNET: downloading and showing AdMob ads, and opening connections between the players' devices for multiplayer (which stays on the local network and does not use the internet);
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: knowing whether the device is on a network (also for ads) and on a Wi-Fi network, to warn you when it isn't;
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE: receiving match announcements broadcast on the local network;
  • WAKE_LOCK: keeping the Wi-Fi connection low-latency during a multiplayer match;
  • VIBRATE: vibrating the phone as game feedback (for example when you are hit); it collects no data;
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: lets AdMob read Android's advertising ID to show and measure ads, where allowed (see «Advertising»).

Same-Wi-Fi multiplayer

When you host or join a match, the devices talk directly to each other over the local network:

  • the host broadcasts on the local network an announcement with the match's three-letter code, the number of players, the map size and the game version;
  • during the match the devices exchange the match state and game commands.

To communicate, the devices need to know each other's local IP address: the other participants, and anyone connected to the same network, can therefore see your device's local IP address. The app does not exchange usernames, accounts, device identifiers or any other personal content.

This data does not reach me or any external server and is not kept after the match. Communication on the local network is not encrypted: I recommend playing on networks you trust, because on a public Wi-Fi other connected people could see the match announcements and try to join.

Advertising

In the Android app the game shows full-screen ads through Google AdMob, at some points between matches. They do not appear during a match or in the tutorial. If there is no connection or no ad available, the game simply carries on without one.

What data. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and (where allowed) personalize them, Google's SDK may collect and process directly from your device: Android's advertising ID, the IP address, device and app data (for example model, Android version, app version and language), ad interactions (impressions and clicks), diagnostic information and an approximate location derived from the IP address. I do not receive this data: from AdMob I only get aggregate statistics about ads and revenue.

Who processes it. For this data Google (and its advertising partners) acts as an independent controller, under its own policy: see Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services. The list of data collected by the SDK is in Google's documentation for the Data safety section. Google may process this data outside the European Economic Area as well, with the safeguards described in its policy.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's consent form appears at startup, before ads are requested. If you do not give the necessary consent, the app does not request ads. You can change your choice at any time from the Ad options button on the main screen, which appears when Google requires it.

Controls on your device. You can reset the advertising ID or turn off ad personalization from Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads) and manage your Google account's preferences at adssettings.google.com.

How I use data and who it is shared with

In short, for each type of data:

  • Data collected by me or sent to me: none.
  • Progress and settings (on the device only): I use them solely so you can resume the game where you left off, with your preferences; I share them with no one.
  • Local IP address and match data (multiplayer only, on the local network only): they are needed only to run the match; I share them only with the other participants in the match and with devices on the same network, and they are not kept.
  • Advertising data (advertising ID, IP address, device and app data, ad interactions, diagnostics, approximate location): collected by the Google AdMob SDK directly from the device to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, with consent where required, personalize them; I do not receive it.
  • Types of parties the data is shared with: Google (AdMob) and its advertising partners, for advertising only. There are no analytics or crash-reporting providers, cloud services of mine or data brokers.

Sharing with third parties

I do not sell your data. Apart from the advertising described above, I do not hand over or share data with third parties; advertising is run by Google under its own policy.

If you install the app from Google Play, Google processes some data (for example installs and updates and, if you turned it on, diagnostics) under its own privacy policy. From the Play Console I only receive aggregated statistics, such as the number of installs and any reported crashes.

Children

Space Warfront is not aimed at children and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are shown by Google AdMob in line with Google Play's and Google's rules on the target audience. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the app, you can reset the device's advertising ID from Android settings and write to me at marco.lombardo@gmail.com.

Retention, deletion and security

Retention: I keep no data on servers or systems of mine. The data saved on the device (see above) stays there until you delete it, and what is exchanged in multiplayer is not kept after the match. Data processed by Google for advertising is kept by Google under its own policy.

Deletion: you can delete the saved data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data from Android settings. Since there are no accounts and no data on my systems, there is no account or data deletion procedure to request from me; for advertising data you can reset the advertising ID and use the Google controls listed above.

Security: the data saved on the device is protected by Android's app sandbox. No personal data travels to me; traffic on the local network between the players' devices is not encrypted, but it carries only match state and game commands, not personal data (the local IP address is, in any case, known to the network itself). Communication with AdMob is handled by Google's SDK.

Your rights

Since I do not process your personal data on my systems, there is nothing I can provide, correct or delete; the app has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. For the data Google processes for advertising, the rights granted by the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent) are exercised with Google, using the tools described in its privacy policy, and consent can be withdrawn from the «Ad options» button or from Android settings. You can also write to me at marco.lombardo@gmail.com, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Under California privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA), I do not sell personal information. Sending data to Google for advertising may count as «sharing» for behavioral advertising: you can limit it by turning off ad personalization in Android or Google settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects data (for example with purchases, accounts, new analytics tools or new ad formats), I will update this page and the date at the top before release, together with the data safety declaration on Google Play.
