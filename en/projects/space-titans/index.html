Game

Space Titans

Real-time tactical battles between a few enormous ships.

Privacy policy

Space Titans is a real-time space tactics game for Android, with enormous ships drawn in great detail. You gather no resources and build no ships: you command a small fleet that is already deployed, five ships against five at most, and you win with manoeuvres.

You turn the ship to present the shield that is still charged, keep the enemy on the flank where all the batteries fire, hit the engines to slow it down or the weapons to silence it, and use the battleship's Cannon or the shield dome at the right moment. With the tactical pause you can stop time and draw the exact path your ships will follow.

The Campaign has 30 missions in 5 acts, from the Kessar border to the throne of the Vhar emperor, with escorts, station defences, ambushes and comic-style dialogue; the stars you earn upgrade your ships in Research. There are also Battle, to choose fleets, difficulty and battlefield, and same-Wi-Fi multiplayer for 2 to 4 players, free-for-all or in an Alliance against the AI.

What you can do
  • Command a fleet of enormous ships: frigates, cruisers, battleships, carriers, shield ships and a flagship
  • Play 30 missions in 5 acts: destroy enemy fleets, escort a convoy or defend a station
  • Manage shields split into four sectors, armor and the subsystems you can hit: engines, shields, weapons and bridge
  • Draw the exact path your ships follow and use the tactical pause to plan your moves
  • Spend your stars on Research to upgrade firepower and shields of each ship class, up to +30%
  • Browse the database of every Coalition and Vhar ship
  • Play a free Battle choosing your fleet, the enemy's, the battlefield, the difficulty and whether to bring your upgrades
  • Challenge friends on the same Wi-Fi, 2 to 4 players, free-for-all or in an Alliance against the AI
