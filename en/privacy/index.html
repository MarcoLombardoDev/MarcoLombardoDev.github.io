Privacy & cookies

What this site stores, and why

This site is static: no tracking cookies, no traffic analytics, no advertising, no data shared with third parties.

What's stored. The only information kept in your browser is the language you've chosen (Italian or English), via localStorage — a technology similar to cookies, but private to this domain. It only serves to show you the page in the right language on your next visit, and it's a value you set yourself by choosing IT or EN.

This value is never sent to a server, doesn't identify you, and stays only on your device until you clear it. Because of that, it doesn't require consent: it falls under what EU law calls strictly necessary technical storage, exempt under Italy's Privacy Code and the ePrivacy Directive.

Clearing it. You can clear it at any time from your browser's settings (clear site data for this domain), or by browsing in a private window.
