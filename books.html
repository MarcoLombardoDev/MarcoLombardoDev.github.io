How I build

Four rules I keep coming back to

01

Start from the problem

Content is the answer, never the question. If the problem isn't clear, no format — toolkit, guide or book — will save it.

02

Ship, then refine

A real release beats a perfect outline. Real readers give better feedback than any draft.

03

One clear job

Generic advice helps nobody. Every toolkit, guide or book targets one real problem for one real audience.

04

Guaranteed, always

Every product carries a money-back guarantee. If it doesn't deliver, you don't pay. Simple.
